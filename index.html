What to check before you order

PonyBuy product links

Save the original Weidian product link, Taobao listing URL, or 1688 seller page when available. Record the marketplace item ID, seller name, exact color option, and date you checked stock. If an agent’s link preview opens, compare its photos and options with the original listing before selecting a variant.

PonyBuy finds by size and fit

Start with the seller’s shoe size chart and check whether its centimeters refer to foot length or insole length. For a hoodie, compare chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length, and body length with a garment you already own. For pants, compare the flat waist measurement, rise, and inseam. Measure head circumference for caps.

PonyBuy QC photos

Use warehouse inspection photos of your own item to check the selected size label and color, then look for outsole wear, uneven seams, zipper alignment, and clasp condition where relevant. Request a close-up or a photo with a ruler if the detail you need is unclear. Check the seller’s return window before approving shipment.

PonyBuy shipping guide

Ask for packed dimensions, actual parcel weight, and the shipping line’s rules for volumetric weight. Compare the estimated total delivered cost, including domestic delivery and agent service fees where applicable. Check destination restrictions and import charges before deciding whether to consolidate multiple items.

Frequently asked questions

What is a PonyBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that organizes product links and notes by category. A row can record the seller, item options, measurements and available QC photos. Check the live listing before ordering.

Where do the category links take me?

The category links open CAFANS catalog pages. Confirm the original marketplace listing and whether your chosen shopping agent accepts its URL before you pay.

Can QC photos authenticate a branded item?

Warehouse QC photos help check visible condition, color and measurements. For authenticity, buy through an authorized retailer or use an independent authentication service where available.

Does the listed product price include international shipping?

Check the item price and the international shipping quote separately. The final total can also include local delivery, agent fees and import charges, depending on the destination.
